# Clash Royale Deck Coaching

Personal coaching for one player's Clash Royale decks, based on their observed battles and card collection.

## Language

**Deck**:
An exact set of eight cards. Swapping a single card produces a different Deck.
_Avoid_: Deck variant, build

**Regular deck**:
A Deck observed in a non-war battle, such as Ladder.
_Avoid_: Ladder deck, normal deck

**War deck**:
A Deck observed in a clan war (river race) battle. Its record is kept separate from a Regular deck with the same eight cards.

**Main deck**:
The Regular deck the player has declared they currently play. It changes only when the player says so or starts an Experiment on it (the swap is applied to the Main deck at once), never because another Deck was played more.
_Avoid_: Best deck, most-played deck

**Collection**:
Every card the player owns, with its current level.
_Avoid_: Cards, inventory

**War deck plan**:
Four Decks built from the Collection with no card in more than one of them. Its Decks need not have been played yet.
_Avoid_: War lineup, war set

**Active war deck plan**:
The War deck plan the player has declared they currently use in war. It is followed by its Decks' records, not by Experiments.

Clan war rules let the player play each of their four war attacks with a different Deck, and each Deck only once per day — so a weak Deck can't be worked around by repeating a stronger one on the same day; it needs a replacement Deck instead. A war week also has only 4 Battle Days where attacks count (the other days are Training Days, Gold only, no Medals); each Deck's four cards can't be reused across that day's other Decks either, which matches a War deck plan having no card in more than one Deck. (Source: timesaver.gg and guildorder.com Clan Wars 2 guides, accessed 2026-10-25; community-sourced, not confirmed against Supercell's own pages.)

**Meta deck**:
A Deck that a named external source reports as popular or strong at a stated date. It is a comparison point, never evidence about the player's own results.
_Avoid_: Best deck, top deck

## Coaching

**Suggestion**:
A proposed change to a Deck, offered by the coach. It becomes an Experiment only when the player chooses to test it.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, tip

**Experiment**:
A card swap on the Main deck that the player is testing (cards out, cards in), with a start date and a success criterion. It is judged on every battle after the start date played with the Main deck of that moment, where the cards in are present and the cards out are absent. Experiments may overlap and share battles.

**Verdict**:
The outcome of an Experiment once its success criterion can be judged: succeeded, failed, or inconclusive. A Verdict never changes the Main deck by itself.
_Avoid_: Result, conclusion
_Avoid_: Test, trial

**Battle history**:
Every battle observed for the player, kept over time rather than only the latest battle log window.
_Avoid_: Battle log, sample
